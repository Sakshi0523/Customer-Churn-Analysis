# Customer-Churn-Analysis
End-to-end analysis of 19K+ customer records using SQL for data ingestion and Power BI for retention strategy visualization.
## 🎯 Problem Statement
A telecom company was experiencing high customer churn with no clear visibility into *who* was leaving or *why*. This project identifies at-risk segments and recommends data-driven retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Technical Skills
* **SQL:** Data cleaning & ingestion of 19,250+ records.
* **Power BI:** Interactive dashboarding & data storytelling.
* **DAX:** Custom measures for Churn % & Retention impact.
* **Analysis:** Customer segmentation & tenure risk profiling.

## 📈 Executive Summary of Findings
After analyzing 19,250 customer records, the following key drivers for churn were identified:

1. **The "Danger Zone":** Customers in the 6-12 month tenure bracket are at the highest risk.
2. **Technical Bottlenecks:** Fiber Optic users churn at 41.1%, suggesting a need for service-level optimization or targeted loyalty offers in this segment.
3. **Retention Anchors:** Adding 'Online Security' to a customer's plan reduces churn probability from 44.2% to 15.0%.
4. **Revenue Stability:** Month-to-month contracts and manual payment methods (Mailed Checks) are the leading predictors of revenue loss.
